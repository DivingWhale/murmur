Murmur

UI for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.54 (240×240), shown at 2×. Use the buttons below the screen, or tap rows on the screen. Supports English and Chinese.

Buttons (proposed)

PLUS
Start recording right away. Press again to stop and save. Works from any screen, pauses a sync in progress, and wakes the device from sleep straight into recording. After that recording is saved and synced, it goes back to sleep.
NEXT
Press: move the highlight down Today, then onto the When I Have Time line (one stop), then back to the top. In “Move to Today?”: press to see the next When I Have Time task. Hold 0.8 s: sleep straight away (screen off). While asleep: press to wake to the lock screen.
OK
On a Today task: check it off (or undo). On the When I Have Time line: opens “Move to Today?” for the task shown; press again to move it into Today. Hold 0.8 s: back one level — “Move to Today?” → Today → lock screen → sleep. The screen shows where you’re going; release early to cancel.
Lock
Cycles through Miscellaneous notes chosen on the computer. OK unlocks, NEXT shows the next note, PLUS records. Swipe left/right (≥ 24 px ≈ 2.8 mm) to change notes; swipe up (≥ 60 px ≈ 7 mm, a quarter of the screen) to unlock. Taps are ignored, so it stays safe in a pocket.
Panel
Swipe down from the top edge of Today or a page: Wi-Fi status, Sync now, language, a big brightness bar (20 levels — tap or drag), All settings. Swipe up or OK closes it. In Settings, swipe up/down to scroll, tap a row to change it, swipe right or hold OK to go back. NEXT/OK step through rows.
Pages
Touch only. On Today, swipe left/right (anywhere except the bottom line) for Today → Claude usage → MD simulations. On a page, OK or NEXT returns to Today; hold OK = lock, hold NEXT = sleep, PLUS records.
Touch
Tap a task to check it off. Swipe left/right on the bottom line to browse When I Have Time (the rotation stops; it resumes after 10 s idle). Tapping the bottom line opens “Move to Today?” with large Move and Cancel buttons. It cancels by itself after 8 s, so a stray touch never moves anything.
